# BT-05 — Starting-point proposal

**Mode:** 👤 HITL — *it moves your real menu bar items*
**needs:** BT-04 · **blocks:** BT-14

## Why
After granting the permission everything is still "Visible" and BarT visibly does nothing. The
proposal is what shows the point of the app in the one moment the user is paying attention
(PRD §3.1, R4-Q6 = "propose, apply on request").

## Slice
Controller logic (which items are "system essentials") → welcome window step → real drags.

## Visible result
Press "Apply" and the menu bar visibly empties down to clock, battery, Wi-Fi and Control Center.

## Done when
- Final step of the welcome window proposes moving everything except clock, battery, Wi-Fi and
  Control Center to "Hidden", and names the affected items.
- Applies **only** on "Apply"; "Set up myself" is an equal-weight option, not fine print.
- Applying runs through `moveItem`, so the oscillation brake and error path stay intact.

## Files
`BarT/UI/WelcomeView.swift`, `BarT/Engine/MenuBarController.swift`

## Acceptance needs you to
Watch one run and confirm the selection is sensible on your bar.
