# BT-12 — Auto-collapse after 15 seconds

**Mode:** 🤖 AFK* — *I can trigger reveal and screenshot the bar 16 s later; if the synthetic
trigger fails I need one click from you*
**needs:** — · **blocks:** BT-14

## Why
Reveal, get distracted, and the bar stays open. Fixed at 15 s, deliberately not configurable
(R4-Q4, PRD §3.3).

## Slice
Controller timer → existing collapse path → menu bar.

## Visible result
Reveal, wait, and the bar closes on its own.

## Done when
- A reveal starts a 15 s timer; expiry runs the existing `collapse()`.
- Interaction in the menu bar restarts the timer.
- Collapsing by click or shortcut cancels it.
- The timer never fires against an already collapsed bar (the existing guard covers this — keep it
  covered).
- Applies to ⌥-reveal as well.
