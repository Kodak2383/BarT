# BT-11 — Configurable shortcut

**Mode:** 👤 HITL — *a system-wide combination has to be pressed to be trusted*
**needs:** — · **blocks:** BT-14

## Why
⌃⌥⌘B is hard-wired. On a collision the settings say "already taken" and offer nothing — a dead end
for a public release. It also gives the General tab a second real setting instead of explanatory
text (R3-Q1).

## Slice
Recorder field → persistence → Carbon re-registration → live hotkey.

## Visible result
Click the field, press a combination, release: it is registered immediately and works from another
app.

## Done when
- A recorder field captures a combination and persists it.
- The hotkey re-registers live — no restart.
- A combination Carbon refuses produces a readable message and keeps the previous one.
- Reset to ⌃⌥⌘B is available.
- Exactly one shortcut (R4-Q7); ⌥-reveal stays a click gesture.

## Files
`BarT/Services/GlobalHotKey.swift`, `BarT/UI/SettingsView.swift`, settings persistence

## Acceptance needs you to
Press a few combinations, including a deliberately occupied one (⌘Space).
