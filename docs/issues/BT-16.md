# BT-16 — Teach the ⌘-drag

**Mode:** 👤 HITL — *whether the explanation lands is a judgement call*
**needs:** BT-15 · **blocks:** BT-14

## Why
With BT-15, arranging items becomes the user's job (PRD §3.2). Almost nobody knows macOS lets you
⌘-drag menu bar items — an app that relies on the gesture has to teach it, or it looks broken.

## Slice
Welcome window + items view + README, one explanation in three places at the right moments.

## Visible result
Someone who has never heard of ⌘-drag knows what to do within a minute of first launch.

## Done when
- The welcome window shows the gesture as a step, in one sentence plus an illustration or short
  animation: "Hold ⌘ and drag an item across the separator."
- The items view repeats it compactly where it matters — the section explanations are the place.
- The README covers it with the screenshot.
- The wording says what the separators *are*, since the user is aiming at them.

## Files
`BarT/UI/WelcomeView.swift`, `BarT/UI/SettingsView.swift`, `README.md`

## Acceptance needs you to
Read it once as if you did not know the gesture.
